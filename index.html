About

The auto-reply is on. I'm somewhere with a view.

Melanie Hedrick has been to 27 countries and still has a mailing address, a calendar, and a life she genuinely likes coming home to. She travels solo through Europe and beyond — champagne on a flower-draped London terrace, paragliding over the French Alps, sunset swims off the Croatian coast — and brings back the details that make it doable: where to stay, what it actually costs, and which splurges are worth every euro.

This is travel for women who've run the meetings, the carpools, and the group chat, and would now like the room with the balcony, thank you. Not a gap year. Not a someday list. Just well-planned time out of office, taken now.
